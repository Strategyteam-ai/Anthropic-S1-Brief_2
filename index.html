$33B 9M’26 revenue vs ~$900B of compute committed through 2045

S-1 Review - Key takeaways

The $104.0B 9M’26 net loss is ~90% non-cash (convertible-note revaluation); adjusted operating margin +12%

What leadership needs to know

Who is the customer?

Enterprise-led: 11,900+ pay $100K+ a year (vs ~950 a year ago); 1,900+ pay $1M+; ~250 pay $10M+. Top 5 = ~25% of revenue, none >10%.

What do they sell?

Claude models via Claude Platform (API), plus agents Claude Code (coding) and Claude Cowork (knowledge work).

Business model

79% pay-per-token ($26.3B), 21% subscriptions ($6.9B) in 9M’26; ~44% sold via AWS, Google Cloud, Azure.

Growth

9M’26 revenue $33.2B vs $2.5B in 9M’25. Q3’26: $17.0B (+47% vs Q2’26), but back to a $567M operating loss after Q2’26’s $974M profit.

Expected revenue

No guidance in S-1. Filed: Q3’26 revenue $17.0B. If Q4 grows at Q3’s +47%, FY26 ≈ $58B (our estimate); ~$600M given up to price cuts in Q3.

Unit cost (cost to serve)

9M’26: ~$14.5 of every $100 earned went on serving customers ($4.8B of $33.2B) → ~85% gross margin (9M’25: ~29%). Opus 5.5: ~40% cheaper per task than Opus 5.

Model cost (cost to train)

9M’26 total compute $18.8B (57% of revenue; 172% in 9M’25). Less $4.8B serving cost ≈ $14.0B on training, research & free users.

Debt

Light: $6.2B Amazon notes; $15B revolver undrawn. Real leverage: ~$895B of compute commitments: ~$448B due before 2030, ~$70B in the next 12 months.

Margins: now vs 2027

9M’26: ~85% gross (derived), –4% operating, +12% adjusted. Q3’26 slipped to –3% as $5.0B of costly short-term compute hit; filing expects more near-term pressure.

Source: Anthropic Form S-1, filed Oct 26, 2026, and S-1/A, filed Nov 2, 2026. Margins marked “derived” are our arithmetic. Tap the i buttons for explanations.
